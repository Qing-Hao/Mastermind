Not live — retrying
Dropped — the socket closed and is retrying with backoff (1s, 2s, 4s, capped). Everything still works: this is a page that has stopped hearing about other people's edits, not a page that has stopped saving.

Caught up
Back — messages were missed while it was down, so reconnecting reloads everything rather than resuming. The badge fades after a few seconds.
Open question for you. Is the dropped badge worth its space at your size, or would you rather the page said nothing and simply caught up when it could? Saying nothing is fewer moving parts; saying something is the difference between "stale" and "quietly wrong".
