What the numbers say

Viewport zoom buys nothing measurable. Magnifying the picture magnifies the overlap with it. It is worth having as the pan-and- peer gesture, and it is not the fix.

Layout zoom is the fix — 25 collisions down to 2 by 2.5×. Its cost is real: at 2.5× the canvas is 3000px wide and roughly a third of the map is on screen at once, so the thing the map is for — the whole department in one look — is only true at 1×.

Semantic labelling is what makes 1× usable, and it is the cheapest of the three: no zoom, no scrolling, 25 down to 16 by dropping 18 labels that say “future direction” next to a circle already drawn dashed. Worth shipping on its own, before any zoom.
