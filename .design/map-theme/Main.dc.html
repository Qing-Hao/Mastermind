What the bezel is for

It answers the map's own question — where is the team pointed now — without reading a word. 12 ticks in, 18 out, and which arms they belong to. Today that count exists nowhere on the page.

What it costs

Tick direction is one new thing to learn, so it takes a line of key. Rotated arm names read slower than level ones, and radius now means depth plus a lane rather than depth alone.

Two things to decide

The plate is set in IBM Plex Sans Condensed and Plex Mono. The app ships no webfont and has no build step, so this is optional — the same design holds in system-ui, a little wider and a little less instrument. And a dark plate still means re-picking eight hues: the palette's 3:1 floor was measured on white.
