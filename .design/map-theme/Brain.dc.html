Direction B
Brain — lobes, clusters, synapses

No rings and no centre. Each root track is a lobe, each subtrack a cluster packed inside it, and every project a cell placed by phyllotaxis — so circles cannot collide by construction rather than by a sweep. The 11 dependencies are drawn always, as synapses, instead of waiting for a hover.

What it fixes

Packing beats a ring for 30 nodes: a lobe holds its cells at whatever density it needs, and a one-project track no longer gets a wedge of the whole circle. Dependencies become part of the picture rather than something you have to go looking for.

What it costs

The centre goes, and with it the one thing the map currently says without a legend: distance from the hub is distance from being committed to. A lobe says which track; nothing says how far along.

And the labels still collide

Packing solves circles, not text. Inside a 20-cell lobe the names are tighter than on the ring, which is why idea names are off here too. The label problem is the same problem in a different shape.
