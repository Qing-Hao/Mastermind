Where the crowding comes from

18 of the 30 projects are ideas, and every idea sits on one ellipse at radius 1.0 — the outermost, longest-labelled, least-differentiated ring on the map. Half the label ink is spent on the row that carries the least information.

Why more pixels are not the answer alone

The canvas grows to 860px tall and stops, so past about 1550px of width the rings stretch flatter rather than rounder. Width buys arc; height does not follow it.

Label width is never an input

A wedge is sized by project count. A one-project track with a long name gets the same slice as a one-project track called Mobile, at the radius where the arc is shortest.
