The switch, and the one thing the two modes do not share

The switch goes last in the map's own control row, after a spacer — the same slot the Sprint tab's Rendered | Raw switch occupies. Department, tier and track govern both drawings. Status does not: each mode opens on its own default, because the two ask different questions of the same rows.

What the roadmap mode must not do
No points summed per quarter — a per-window total is the points-per-day constant the capacity design rules out. Counts of projects and checkpoints are safe.
No drag-to-quarter rescheduling. A quarter column is 13 weeks wide; a drop into it would have to invent a date, and dates belong to the user.
