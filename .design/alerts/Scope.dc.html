A count derived from dates already on screen is a readout, the same genus as the offline badge beside it. It becomes a notification system the moment it remembers anything about a person — and the gate stores nothing about anyone by design.
Stays inside the line

Everyone sees the same list. Nothing is addressed to anyone.
Reports only. A late phase stays late until someone moves the date or closes the work — nothing reschedules.

Email, desktop push, or a toast that interrupts — an external integration and a feed.
