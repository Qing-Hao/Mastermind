Single sign-on

When on, every page requires a Keycloak session. Mastermind stores no password, creates no account and keeps no record of who you are — the session is a signed cookie and nothing else.

Turning it on signs you in first. The switch flips only if the round trip comes back with an identity this page allows, so a wrong setting here cannot lock you out — it simply refuses to arm.

Keycloak

The first three come from your realm's discovery document and its client. The secret is the one value on this page that is one.

The client is confidential, so the secret is what makes the back-channel exchange possible. Stored: unknown. Typing a new one replaces it; Clear removes it and falls back to MASTERMIND_OIDC_SECRET in the server's environment, if that is set.

It is stored in roadmap.db. The export strips it, the file does not — clear it before sharing the database or a backup.

If you are locked out

      

        Start the server with MASTERMIND_SSO=off
        in its environment. The gate is skipped for that process, this page comes
        back, and everything configured above is left exactly as it was. That is the
        answer for a rotated secret, a deleted client, a realm that is down and a
        VPN that is off.
